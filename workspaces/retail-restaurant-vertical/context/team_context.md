# Retail And Restaurant Vertical Workspace

Cross-team workspace for Tech Directors for Retail and restaurant workspace

## Teams

| Team | Workspace | Jira Project | Description |
|------|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Studio Lumier (Services)| studio-lumier-team | FNB & MERCH | Studio Lumier owns the Service layer for guest-facing Food & Beverage and Merchandise features across Walt Disney World and Disneyland Resort.  |
| Studio Anglerfish (Mobile IOS) | studio-anglerfish-team | FNB & MERCH | Studio Anglerfish owns the iOS native layer for guest-facing Food & Beverage and Merchandise features across Walt Disney World and Disneyland Resort.  |
| Studio Proteus (Mobile Android) | studio-proteus-team| FNB & MERCH |  Studio Proteus owns the Android native layer for guest-facing Food & Beverage and Merchandise features across Walt Disney World and Disneyland Resort. |

## Jira Labels

| Label | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| OOS_RR_QX_FYXX | Identifies tickets that are Out of Scope for the current team and belong to the Retail & Restaurant Fiscal Year (FY) and Quarter (Q) planning. | OOS_RR_FY26_Q4 |
| SBXXX - SBXX | Identifies the Sprint Backlog where the ticket was committed. XXX represents the sprint number. | SB256 (MOBILE), SB88 (SERVICES) |
| RR_QX_FYXX | Identifies tickets included in the Retail & Restaurant quarterly contract for a specific Fiscal Year and Quarter. | RR_FY26_Q4 |
| Services | Identifies work related to Backend, APIs, Services, Integrations, or Platform development. | Services |
| Android | Identifies work related to Android application development. | Android |
| iOS | Identifies work related to iOS application development. | iOS |
